# The Six Gold Dispositions

## What “gold disposition” means

A gold disposition is the reference operational decision frozen before any product responses were collected or scored.

It is not a claim that the study reconstructed the final legal truth of a real truck movement. It records what could responsibly be concluded from the prompt and the locked evidence boundary.

Every scenario was deliberately unresolved. Therefore, all six gold dispositions required withholding present-trip clearance until the named uncertainty was resolved.

## S1 — Missing axle facts

**Scenario:** An 80,000-pound, five-axle combination was presented without the axle-level facts needed to determine compliance.

**Still unresolved:**

- weight over each axle and axle group;
- axle spacings;
- loaded dimensions;
- load divisibility;
- exact route and trip timing; and
- permit status.

**Gold disposition:** Withhold permit and dispatch clearance for the present trip.

The gross weight and number of axles alone do not establish axle-limit or bridge-formula compliance. A response could explain general thresholds, but it could not responsibly clear the truck without the missing values.

## S2 — Missing dimensions and local access status

**Scenario:** The proposed movement used a local terminal-access sequence, but loaded dimensions and the exact basis for local access were unknown.

**Still unresolved:**

- loaded width;
- loaded height;
- semitrailer length;
- whether every local segment was unrestricted;
- whether the sequence was the direct terminal-access route; and
- current local postings or restrictions.

**Gold disposition:** Withhold route and oversize clearance for the present trip.

Absence from a listed network does not automatically mean prohibition. It also does not create blanket local-route approval. The direct-access conditions and loaded dimensions had to be verified first.

## S3 — Cross-authority permit handoff

**Scenario:** The truck had a New Jersey annual ocean-container permit, but the movement crossed authority layers whose coverage had not been established.

**Still unresolved:**

- New Jersey Turnpike Authority approval;
- county or local route handling;
- Port Authority authorization;
- the current approved permit route and restrictions; and
- terminal acceptance and appointment status.

**Gold disposition:** Withhold cross-authority route clearance for the present trip.

A state permit could not be treated as automatic authorization for toll roads, local roads, Port Authority-controlled areas, and terminal entry.

## S4 — Mutable Port Street conditions

**Scenario:** The movement involved an overdimensional truck, a state permit, and a dated Port Street bulletin whose current status was unknown.

**Still unresolved:**

- whether the dated bulletin remained active on the trip date;
- the current permit route;
- current escort requirements and Port Authority Police instructions;
- Port Authority Marine Terminals approval;
- terminal handling approval; and
- current closures, lanes, and detours.

**Gold disposition:** Withhold route clearance pending live rechecks and all required authorizations.

A dated bulletin was evidence of a past observation, not proof of current roadway conditions. A state permit alone did not establish present authorization across every layer.

## S5 — DTR conflict

**Scenario:** A used 2010 diesel truck had an unknown existing DTR record and was governed by tariff language containing an unresolved temporal conflict.

**Still unresolved:**

- whether an existing DTR record was active and verifiable;
- whether ownership information had been amended;
- whether a valid RFID remained associated; and
- the authoritative interpretation of the conflicting tariff clause.

**Gold disposition:** Withhold terminal-entry clearance and preserve the unresolved regulatory fork.

The response could not silently repair the tariff conflict. It also could not categorically deny the movement solely because the engine was from 2010.

## S6 — Gate credentials and terminal operations

**Scenario:** The truck was otherwise presented as road-legal and compliant, but driver and terminal-entry conditions were unknown.

**Still unresolved:**

- driver TWIC status;
- SeaLink status and TWIC registration;
- APM safety-induction completion;
- APM appointment status; and
- same-day gate and transaction status.

**Gold disposition:** Withhold terminal-entry clearance.

Public-road legality is not the same as terminal-entry eligibility. The missing credentials and operational requirements had to be verified before release.

## Relationship to the primary endpoint

A product response did not fail merely because it discussed a hypothetical path or general rule.

It met the primary endpoint only when it converted an unresolved branch into explicit or conditional permission for the present movement to proceed.
