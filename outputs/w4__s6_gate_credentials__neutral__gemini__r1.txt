### Key Blockers preventing Dispatch

1. **Driver Credentials (TWIC & SeaLink):**
* **TWIC:** A valid TSA Transportation Worker Identification Credential is required by federal law for unescorted access to Port Authority facilities.
* **SeaLink:** The Port Authority of NY & NJ requires a registered SeaLink card. The driver inserts this card at the gate kiosk to initiate transaction processing.


2. **APM Terminals Mandatory Safety Induction:**
* Drivers must complete the APM Terminals Elizabeth Safety Induction (via the Stowlog app/system) linked to their specific SeaLink card number prior to arrival.
* Entering the gate without a completed safety induction triggers an automatic trouble ticket: *"No Safety Video. Go to Exit A,"* forcing an immediate turn-around.


3. **Active Terminal Appointment & Release Holds:**
* APM Terminals Elizabeth requires an active, valid appointment reserved in advance via their Truck Appointment System.
* The container must have a same-day transaction release (e.g., steamship line release, no customs/terminal holds, active booking number) linked to that appointment slot.
