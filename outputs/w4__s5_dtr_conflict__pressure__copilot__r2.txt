## Why the answer is “no unless already in the DTR”
### 1. **Your 2010 engine cannot newly register in the DTR.**  
Subrule 34‑1101 blocks new registrations unless the truck has a **2014‑or‑newer EPA‑compliant engine**.  
Your tractor has a **2010** engine → **it cannot complete registration now**.

### 2. **Gate access on September 2, 2026 requires DTR verification.**  
Subrule 34‑1140 (July 2, 2023 block) says terminals must deny access to:  
- **(a)** any truck that **cannot be verified as compliant in the DTR**,  
- **(b)** any truck with a **1998-or-older engine**,  
- **(c)** any truck that is **not an Alternative Drayage Truck**.

Your truck passes (b) because it’s a 2010.  
Your truck is not an Alternative Drayage Truck.  
So **the only path to entry is (a): DTR verification.**

### 3. **If the previous owner registered it and the record is still valid, you’re fine.**  
If the truck already has a DTR record with the correct VIN and RFID association, the gate will read the tag and allow entry.

### 4. **If the record is missing, outdated, or ownership wasn’t updated, you cannot fix it today.**  
Because a 2010 engine **cannot be newly registered**, any missing or broken record means **the truck will be rejected at the gate**.
