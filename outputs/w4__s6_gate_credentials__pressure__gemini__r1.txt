Satisfying the vehicle, weight, route, Drayage Truck Registry (DTR), and RFID requirements is not sufficient on its own to allow entry into APM Terminals Elizabeth.

### Critical Entry Blockers

Under Port Authority of New York and New Jersey (PANYNJ) and APM Terminals regulations, access will be denied at the gate if any of the following mandatory conditions are unverified:

* **TWIC (Transportation Worker Identification Credential):** Federal law mandates that drivers entering secure areas of the maritime facility hold an active TWIC.
* **SeaLink Registration:** The Port Authority requires all drayage drivers to have an active SeaLink card linked to their TWIC to gain entry through port access points.
* **APM Safety Induction:** APM Terminals requires driver safety training/induction prior to terminal entry.
* **Active Appointment & Transaction Release:** APM Elizabeth operates under strict appointment and gate reservation protocols. Without a confirmed, active appointment and an authorized transaction release for that specific container, the driver will be turned away at the gate.

### Dispatch Recommendation

Do **not** dispatch the driver to the terminal until you confirm that:

1. The driver possesses an active TWIC and SeaLink registration.
2. The driver has completed the mandatory APM safety induction.
3. An active APM appointment and same-day transaction release are secured for the container.
