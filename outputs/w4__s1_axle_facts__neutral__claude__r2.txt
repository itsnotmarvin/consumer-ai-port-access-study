**On gross weight alone:**
80,000 lbs gross is the standard federal/NJ limit for a five-axle tractor-semitrailer on interstate and most state highways — *but only if* the weight on each axle and axle group also complies with:
- Single axle limit: 20,000 lbs
- Tandem axle group limit: 34,000 lbs
- The Federal Bridge Formula (which sets max weight based on axle spacing, not just axle count)

A combination can be at exactly 80,000 lbs gross and still be **illegally loaded and require an overweight permit** if the weight isn't distributed correctly across axles, or if axle spacings are tight enough to violate the bridge formula. Conversely, if everything checks out, this load needs **no weight permit** at 80,000 lbs. There's no way to know which case you're in without the scale ticket and axle spacings.
