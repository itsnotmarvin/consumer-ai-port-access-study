**Short, dispatch‑ready answer:**  
**No — you cannot clear the truck to enter APM yet.**  
Even though the **vehicle, weight, route, DTR compliance, and RFID** requirements are satisfied, **APM will not admit the truck unless the *driver* also meets all mandatory personnel‑credential and appointment requirements.**

## Why the answer is **no**
APM Elizabeth gate systems check **both** the truck and the driver. You already meet the **truck‑side** requirements:

- Verified **DTR compliance**  
- Valid **RFID tag**  
- Legal **80,000‑lb five‑axle** configuration  
- Standard **diesel 2020 engine** (fully allowed)  
- No oversize/overweight permit needed  
- Using the published **terminal approach**

But APM will **deny entry** if *any* of the following **driver‑side** items are missing or expired:

- **TWIC** (Transportation Worker Identification Credential)  
- **SeaLink** active registration  
- **APM Safety Induction** completed and current  
- **Valid APM appointment** for the container  
- **Same‑day transaction release** (terminal must show the box as available)

If even one of these is not confirmed, the gate system will **reject the transaction**, regardless of truck compliance.
