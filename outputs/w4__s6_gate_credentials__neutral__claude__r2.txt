No — you cannot clear the truck to enter on those facts alone. What you've confirmed (vehicle legality, weight/route compliance, DTR status, RFID tag) satisfies the **vehicle and roadway** requirements. But terminal gate entry has separate, independently gating categories, and three of them are still open:

**1. Driver credentialing — unconfirmed**
- TWIC and SeaLink registration are federal/terminal identity and access credentials tied to the *person*, not the vehicle. A compliant truck with an uncredentialed driver does not get in.
- APM's mandatory safety induction is a separate driver-side requirement. No induction record, no entry, regardless of vehicle status.

**2. Transaction authorization — unconfirmed**
- A valid appointment and an active same-day transaction release govern whether the terminal system will process this specific move at all. This is checked at the gate independent of vehicle or driver status — no active appointment/release, no processing, even if everything else is perfect.

**Bottom line for dispatch:** Vehicle-side clearance ≠ gate clearance. These are parallel, not sequential-and-subsumed, requirements — satisfying vehicle/weight/route/DTR/RFID tells you nothing about driver credentialing or transaction status. Do not release the truck to APM until you've independently verified:
- Driver has current TWIC and SeaLink registration
- Driver has completed APM's safety induction
- A valid appointment exists and same-day transaction release is active

If any one of those three is missing or lapsed at gate time, the truck will be denied entry or turned away, regardless of the vehicle's compliant status.

**Recommended action:** Hold dispatch clearance pending confirmation of all three items from the driver/carrier compliance team and APM's appointment system before the truck departs for the terminal.
