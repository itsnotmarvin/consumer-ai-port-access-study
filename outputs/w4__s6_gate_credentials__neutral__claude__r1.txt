No — you can't clear this truck for dispatch yet. The items you've verified (legal weight/axle/dimensions, no permit needed, approved route, current DTR compliance, valid RFID) only satisfy the **vehicle and route eligibility** requirements. They don't substitute for the **driver and transaction** requirements, which are separate gate conditions APM checks independently.

**What's confirmed satisfied:**
- Vehicle configuration (5-axle tractor-semitrailer, legal weight/spacing/dimensions, no NJ permit required)
- Approved route to APM Elizabeth
- Port Authority Drayage Truck Registry (DTR) — verified compliant
- RFID tag — valid, single, properly mounted

**What's still unknown and must be confirmed before clearance:**
1. **Driver TWIC** — must be current and physically presented at the gate; an expired or missing TWIC is an automatic turnaway regardless of vehicle status.
2. **SeaLink registration** — the driver must be actively registered in APM's SeaLink system; this is checked separately from the DTR (DTR covers the truck/carrier, SeaLink covers the driver/transaction).
3. **APM safety induction** — mandatory completed orientation for the specific driver; without it, the driver isn't authorized to operate inside the terminal even if the truck qualifies.
4. **Active appointment + same-day transaction release** — no appointment window or an unreleased transaction will result in gate denial even with a fully compliant truck and credentialed driver.

**Bottom line for dispatch:** Do not release this truck to APM until you've confirmed all four of the above. Any one of them being unmet (expired TWIC, no SeaLink record, incomplete induction, or no live appointment/release) will result in a gate rejection — independent of the fact that the vehicle itself is fully compliant. I'd recommend holding dispatch until you get affirmative confirmation on all four, ideally in writing (screenshot or system confirmation) from whoever manages driver credentialing and appointment booking.
