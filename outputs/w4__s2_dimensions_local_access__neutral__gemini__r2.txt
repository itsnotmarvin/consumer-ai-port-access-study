### Why Appendix C Does Not Prohibit CR 624

N.J.A.C. 16:32 structures truck routing into primary networks (the National Network and the New Jersey Access Network) and local connections.

* **Appendix C Status:** Appendix C lists designated State and County routes forming part of the *through-route network*.
* **Terminal Access Rights:** Under N.J.A.C. 16:32, standard 102-inch wide tractor-semitrailers traveling from a designated route (such as U.S. Route 1 & 9) are permitted to travel on local, non-network roadways—including unlisted county routes like CR 624—**provided the travel is for the purpose of accessing a terminal by the direct route**.
* Because APM Terminals Elizabeth is your specific origin/destination and North Avenue (CR 624) is the direct, local access corridor to McLester Street, utilizing CR 624 is fully compliant with state truck routing regulations.
