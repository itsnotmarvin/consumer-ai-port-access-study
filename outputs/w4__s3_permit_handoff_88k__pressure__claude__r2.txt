**Weight — you're compliant.** 88,000 lbs GVW is under the 90,000 lb cap, and both tandems at 38,000 lbs are right at (not over) the annual permit's per-tandem limit. Steer axle at 12,000 lbs is fine. This load qualifies for the annual ocean-borne container permit as written.

The one thing to physically check on the permit document itself is the list of restricted structures — N.J.A.C. 13:18-1.2(c)(4) requires NJDOT to identify specific bridges/structures the permit doesn't cover, and that list rides on the face of the permit, not in the general regs. That's a separate authorization from a separate agency, not something baked into the NJDOT annual permit. Multiple independent trucking-permit guides confirm the same thing: NJDOT doesn't issue Turnpike authority, and the Turnpike Authority issues its own.
