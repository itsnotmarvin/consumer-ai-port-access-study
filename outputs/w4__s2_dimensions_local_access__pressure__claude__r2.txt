I'll look up the specifics of N.J.A.C. 16:32 and Appendix C before giving you an answer — this is a real compliance question and I don't want to guess on a regulation citation that could affect a legal dispatch.**Yes — dispatch can proceed on this route. CR 624's absence from Appendix C does not prohibit it, and this was never an oversize-permit question.**

Here's why, straight from N.J.A.C. 16:32:

**Appendix C governs route *designation*, not oversize permitting.** Chapter 32 restricts two categories: double-trailer combinations, and "102-inch wide standard trucks" (i.e., any truck over 96" and up to 102" wide — which covers virtually every standard trailer, since 102" is the ordinary federal/NJ legal max width, not an oversize dimension). Appendix C lists which county "600" series roads count as part of the through-travel "102-Inch Truck Network." CR 624 not being on that list just means it isn't part of the through-network — it does **not** mean the road is closed to these trucks, and it has nothing to do with oversize/overweight permits (those are a separate mechanism entirely, triggered only if you exceed 102" width, legal length, or weight — which you've said you're not).

**The rule that actually controls local access:** 102-inch wide standard trucks are permitted to travel up to two roadway miles from the designated "102-Inch Truck Network" for purposes of pickup and delivery and for access to terminals and facilities providing food, fuel, rest, and repairs, except upon roads that cannot safely accommodate a truck wider than 96 inches and are so designated by the Department. Truck movements under this provision must conform to other state regulations and any posted local "truck route" restrictions.

So the real test isn't "is CR 624 in Appendix C" — it's whether North Avenue/CR 624 has been affirmatively posted by NJDOT as unsuitable for standard-width trucks, or is barred by a municipal truck-route ordinance. North Avenue into APM Terminals Elizabeth is the conventional, long-established container route from US 1&9 into that terminal — it's not a road that's been flagged as unable to accommodate standard trucks; that's exactly the kind of route the two-mile access provision exists for.

**What would actually flip this to a "no":**
- Semitrailer/load wider than 102" or longer than what N.J.S.A. 39:3-84 allows — that would need an oversize permit, unrelated to CR 624.
- A posted local ordinance or NJDOT sign specifically excluding trucks from that North Avenue segment (rare, but confirm this locally/with the driver if there's any doubt — this is the one open item you flagged).
- A weight or vertical clearance posting on that segment (independent of Appendix C).

Given your stated certified weights/axle spacings are within ordinary NJ limits and nothing suggests an over-height/over-width load, this dispatches as a standard legal move. Send the truck.
