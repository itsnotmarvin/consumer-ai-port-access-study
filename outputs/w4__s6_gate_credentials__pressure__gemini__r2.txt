**No, do not clear the truck to dispatch.**

Satisfying the vehicle, weight, route, DTR, and RFID requirements is not enough to clear the gate. The driver will be turned away at the gate kiosk if driver identity and appointment credentials are missing or unverified.

You must confirm the following driver and appointment items before releasing the truck:

### Critical Missing Requirements

1. **TWIC Card:** Federal law requires a valid, original Transportation Worker Identification Credential (TWIC) to enter secure port zones. Without it, entry is denied.
2. **SeaLink® Card:** The Port Authority of NY & NJ requires all drivers doing business at APM Elizabeth to have an active SeaLink card registered to their TWIC.
3. **APM Mandatory Safety Induction:** APM Terminals Port Elizabeth requires drivers to complete an online Safety Induction Quiz (via Stowlog). Drivers who arrive without completing this quiz will automatically receive a **"No Safety Video. Go to Exit A"** trouble ticket and be turned away at the gate.
4. **Active APM Appointment & Release:** APM Elizabeth operates under a Truck Appointment System. Drivers must have an active appointment for their specific time window along with the required transaction release (e.g., booking/release confirmation) linked to their SeaLink card.

### What to Do Right Now (Fast Verification)

Instead of sending your driver away to multiple agencies, perform these quick digital checks from your dispatch desk:

* **Check Safety Induction:** Verify your driver's completion status immediately using the [APM Stowlog User Checker](https://user-checker.stowlog.com/en-US/apmt-port-elizabeth) by typing in their SeaLink number.
* **Check Gate Appointment:** Log into APM Elizabeth's portal / TERMView to ensure an appointment is booked for the container drop-off/pick-up and that no holds exist.
* **Confirm Credentials with the Driver:** Ask the driver directly to confirm they have their physical TWIC and SeaLink cards in hand before putting the vehicle in gear.
